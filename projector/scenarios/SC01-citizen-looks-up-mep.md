# SC01 — Citizen Looks Up Their MEP

**Situation:** A citizen wants to know how the MEP(s) representing them actually voted
on legislation, beyond campaign promises or party lines.
**Goal:** Find a specific MEP and see their voting record, attendance, and how closely
they align with their political group, in plain language.
**Pain point:** Today there is no lookup at all — the only output is a static PCA
report with no search, no per-MEP page, and data frozen at the 2014-2019 term.

## Details
Success looks like: search by name or constituency, land on a page showing votes cast,
attendance rate, group-alignment rate, and (per SC02) where they sit in the political
landscape — with a link back to the source data and methodology
(`FR01-provenance-chain`, surfaced via `UC05-inspect-methodology`).
