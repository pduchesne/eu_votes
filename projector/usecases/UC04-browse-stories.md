# UC04 — Engage With Votes by Subject and Story

A citizen without the background to read a PCA plot engages with what Parliament has
been doing on the things they care about.

Split because two different capabilities were hiding under one heading, and conflating
them let an official subject tag pass as "topics" while genuine storytelling quietly
went missing in the rewrite. Deriving what a vote is about is computation with a
measurable answer; explaining why it mattered is editorial work with an author. The
platform needs both, and a reader must be able to tell which is which.
