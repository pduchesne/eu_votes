# UC04.01 — Explore Votes by Derived Topic

A user asks what Parliament did about migration, or climate, or fisheries subsidies, and
gets the votes — including the ones nobody labelled. They can also start from a vote
they care about and ask for others like it.

This rests on semantics derived from the corpus rather than on Parliament's subject
tags, which cover none of the 8th term and around two-thirds of the rest, across seven
top-level subjects. Official tags remain valuable as a check on the derived ones
(`FR04`), not as the means of exploration.
