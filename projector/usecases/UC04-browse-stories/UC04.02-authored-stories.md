# UC04.02 — Read an Authored Story

A citizen reads a short piece written by a person: what a vote was actually about, who
broke ranks, why it mattered — with the figures it cites drawn from the verified data
and every vote it names linked to Parliament's own document.

The reader can always tell they are reading an argument rather than a measurement. That
distinction is the point: this platform's claim is that its numbers are checkable, and
prose that borrows their authority without being checkable would undermine it.
