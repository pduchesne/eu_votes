# UC05 — Inspect Data Provenance and Methodology

From any chart or number in the dashboard, a citizen, journalist, or auditor can trace
back to the raw data source, fetch date, and a plain-language explanation of how the
figure was computed (e.g. what a cohesion score or PCA axis means), so results are
independently verifiable rather than taken on trust.
